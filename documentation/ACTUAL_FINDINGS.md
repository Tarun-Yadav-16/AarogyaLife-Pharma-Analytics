# Validated Findings from the Synthetic Dataset

- Total net sales were approximately **₹23.40 crore**, gross profit was **₹10.48 crore**, and gross margin was **44.77%**.
- Sales increased from **₹11.55 crore in 2024** to **₹11.85 crore in 2025**, approximately **2.62% YoY growth**.
- **South** was the highest-sales region at approximately **₹6.27 crore**; Central was lowest at approximately **₹2.01 crore**.
- **Respiratory** led therapy-area sales at approximately **₹8.86 crore** and gross profit at approximately **₹3.84 crore**.
- **VitalSalbu 12 (P012)** was the highest-sales product at approximately **₹2.20 crore**.
- Retail Pharmacy was the largest channel at approximately **₹4.92 crore**; Government Tender had the longest average payment time at **45.37 days**.
- Company on-time payment performance was **71.74%**, below the **80% target**, indicating a collections opportunity.
- **Mumbai warehouse (W02)** recorded the highest stock-out burden with **683 stock-out days** across the analysis period.
- The dataset contained **420 adverse-event reports**, including **43 serious reports**, **96 cases under review**, and an average reporting delay of **18.9 days**.

## Recommended actions
1. Introduce channel/distributor collection scorecards and focus follow-up on overdue, high-value accounts—especially Government Tender accounts.
2. Reallocate fast-moving respiratory inventory and review reorder points, starting with Mumbai warehouse and top-selling products.
3. Use a controlled safety-operations queue for serious and under-review reports; reporting volume must not be presented as clinical incidence or causality.
